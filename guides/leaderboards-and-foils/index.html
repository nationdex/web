1. Global & Server Leaderboards

Compete against collectors in your server or worldwide! Use /leaderboards nations to view top collectors by total caught nations, or /leaderboards value to rank by total collection net worth.

2. Foil Balance & Economy

Foils are special collectible currencies earned through catches, daily streaks, and trading milestone achievements:

  • Check your current balance: /foils balance
  • Transfer foils to teammates: /foils give
  • Leaderboard to compete: /leaderboards foils

3. Special Drops

Keep an eye out for rare spawn events! Shiny and Special nation variants grant bonus collection score on leaderboards.
